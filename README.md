# Customer Churn Analysis

## Project Overview

This project analyses customer churn data to identify patterns and customer segments associated with customer attrition.

The objective is to use data analysis and visualisation to understand why certain groups of customers are more likely to churn and translate the findings into actionable business recommendations.

## Business Questions

The analysis addresses seven key questions:

1. What does the customer base look like?
2. Which customer segments have the highest churn?
3. Does contract type influence retention?
4. Does tenure affect customer loyalty?
5. Which services are associated with churn?
6. Which payment methods have higher churn?
7. What actions should management take?

## Key Findings

### Contract Type

Month-to-month customers have a churn rate of 42.71%, compared with 11.27% for one-year contracts and 2.83% for two-year contracts.

### Tenure

Customers with 0–12 months of tenure have the highest churn rate at 47.44%, while customers with 61–72 months have a churn rate of 6.61%.

### Internet Service

Fiber optic customers have a churn rate of 41.89%, compared with 18.96% for DSL customers.

### Additional Services

Customers without Online Security have a churn rate of 41.77%, compared with 14.61% among customers with the service.

Customers without Tech Support have a churn rate of 41.64%, compared with 15.17% among customers with Tech Support.

### Payment Method

Electronic check customers have the highest churn rate at 45.29%, compared with 15.24% among customers using automatic credit-card payments.

## Business Risks

1. High churn among customers during their first year.
2. High churn exposure among month-to-month customers.
3. Concentration of churn within identifiable high-risk customer segments.

## Business Opportunities

1. Develop a first-year customer retention programme.
2. Encourage suitable customers to move to longer-term contracts.
3. Increase adoption of relevant value-added services through targeted offers.

## Recommendations

- Introduce structured onboarding and first-year retention initiatives.
- Develop targeted incentives for suitable month-to-month customers.
- Investigate the causes of high churn among fiber optic customers.
- Investigate the electronic-check customer experience.
- Test targeted value-added service bundles.
- Implement a recurring customer churn monitoring dashboard.

## Important Analytical Consideration

The analysis identifies associations between customer characteristics and churn. These relationships should not automatically be interpreted as causal relationships.

For example, the lower churn observed among customers with additional services does not prove that the services themselves cause customers to remain. Further testing would be required to establish causality.

## Project Outcome

The project demonstrates how customer data can be transformed into business insights and actionable recommendations using Excel-based data analysis and visualisation.
